# lobslaw-cluster-claim

**Decision:** Claim CAS compares an FSM-assigned per-record revision, not just ClaimedBy. ScheduledTaskRecord and AgentCommitment carry revision, bumped by the FSM on every write; LOG_OP_CLAIM carries expected_revision (explicitly optional, required, no unconditional default) and is rejected if the stored revision moved. ClaimedBy+ClaimExpiresAt remain, and expiry reinterpretation stays at the scheduler scan layer for replay determinism. Supersedes the ClaimedBy-only CAS.

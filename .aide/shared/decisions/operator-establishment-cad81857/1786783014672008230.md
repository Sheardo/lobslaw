# operator-establishment

**Decision:** Operator authority splits in two. Machine operations are authorised by an mTLS client certificate carrying an operator role, issued by the cluster CA. Access to a person's data is authorised separately, by a principal holding role:operator through the policy engine. A certificate alone never grants the unrestricted audience.
